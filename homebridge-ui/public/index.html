Discovery browses your network for BluOS zones. Multi-zone amplifiers such as the NAD CI S2 and CI 580 appear once per zone, each with its own volume.

HomeKit has no speaker volume control that the Home app draws, so the volume slider is exposed as a fan. Turning it off sets the level to zero; the mute switch is separate.

Use this when multicast is filtered on your network. Leave the port empty to try every documented BluOS port.
